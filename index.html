GLF

Global Logistics Family

Network member

The Global Logistics Family (GLF) is based in the United Kingdom, and emanates from the offices of freight professionals who understand intimately the needs of international, privately owned and managed freight forwarders who want to take their global marketing strength to a higher level with a common identity and a strict ethical and practical code of conduct.

These skills are employed to manage the network, organise professional conferences with one-to-one meetings, and develop targeted reciprocal business.

URS

United Registrar of Systems

URS is a certification body that provides professional, fast, and ethical services.

URS adheres to the principles and requirements of ISO 17021 for auditing and focuses on certification of standards for organizational development and improvement.

URS is the only company that employs only full-time auditors to ensure consistent audit processes and maintain client confidentiality.

URS provides training and development to enhance the knowledge and skills of its personnel, ensuring that audits are conducted in accordance with organizational policies and to maximize benefits for customers.
